What is air duct cleaning and why does it matter?

Air duct cleaning removes dust, pet dander, pollen and debris that build up inside your ductwork. Clean ducts help your home circulate fresher air and ease the load on your heating and cooling system. Flat Rate Air Duct Cleaning NJ provides this air duct cleaning service across Jersey City.

How often should I schedule an air duct cleaning service?

Many homes benefit from air duct cleaning every few years. Schedule sooner if you have pets, allergies, recent renovation work, visible dust at the vents, or if you just moved into a new place. Call 551-233-0219 and we will recommend a schedule that fits your property.

Do you offer air vent cleaning and dryer vent cleaning too?

Yes. Along with air duct cleaning, we clean air vents, registers and grilles, and we perform dryer vent cleaning to clear lint from the vent line. Cleaning them together is an easy way to improve airflow and safety in one appointment.

What is the difference between HVAC cleaning and HVAC duct cleaning?

HVAC duct cleaning focuses on the ductwork that carries air through your property. HVAC cleaning is broader and can include other parts of your heating and cooling system. We will check what your system needs and explain your options before any work begins.
